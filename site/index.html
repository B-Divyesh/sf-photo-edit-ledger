    Sidecar Ledger reads filenames and adjacent XMP. It never decodes image pixels, calls a network, or changes source timestamps.

    --from lightroom --to immich --json

    What the report promises

    Evidence, not false translation.

    01

    Portable

    The destination has a declared mapping for the field and the source can represent it.

    02

    Lossy

    The boundary drops or cannot durably write the field. You get a concrete backup or render recommendation.

    03

    Unknown

    No trustworthy mapping is declared. Sidecar Ledger says so and asks for a representative test.

    Opaque stays opaque. Proprietary develop recipes are named by namespace, never reverse-engineered or printed with their values.
